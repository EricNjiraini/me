## The question

Data teams measure everyone else's performance but rarely their own. Without a few agreed measures, it's hard to show what the team delivers, to spot a growing backlog before stakeholders do, or to make the case for more people.

## Four measures

| Measure | What it tracks |
|---|---|
| Velocity | Tasks completed per sprint |
| Delivery rate | Projects delivered on time, against plan |
| Quality | Defect rate, test coverage and peer-review compliance |
| Utilisation | Time on data work as a share of total capacity |

Each measure has a written definition, so the numbers mean the same thing every week.

## Tools

- **Dashboards:** setup guides for Power BI, for visual reporting, and for Smartsheet, for teams that plan their work there.
- **Weekly team report:** a template for sharing progress with stakeholders.
- **Automation:** Python or R scripts to calculate the measures, with GitHub Issues as the task tracker.

## Why it matters

Clear measures change the conversation with stakeholders, from "the data team is slow" to "here is what we delivered, what's queued, and what it would take to go faster."
