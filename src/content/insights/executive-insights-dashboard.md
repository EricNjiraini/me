## The question

Leadership teams rarely lack data. What they lack is a short, reliable answer to "what changed, why, and what should we do about it?" This project shows the path from raw data to that answer.

## What it produces

- **A KPI dashboard** covering sales and revenue trends, customer behaviour and retention, products and inventory, and regional growth.
- **A five-slide strategy deck** for quarterly planning.
- **An executive summary** with a prioritised list of recommendations.
- **Supporting analysis,** including RFM and cohort analysis of retention.

## One dataset, four audiences

Each executive gets the measures they act on:

| Role | Focus |
|---|---|
| CEO | Revenue, customer lifetime value, growth |
| COO | Fulfilment, inventory, logistics |
| CMO | Acquisition cost, retention, conversion funnel |
| CFO | Cost per order, gross margin, average order value |

## How it's organised

The repository follows the path from data to decision: raw and cleaned data, analysis in R or Python, visuals in Power BI, the storytelling deck, and finally the recommendations.

*The company and figures are illustrative. The data is not from a real business.*
